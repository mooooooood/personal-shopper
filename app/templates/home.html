GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you ask.

Who will I be working with?

You’ll work directly with me. I run Personal Shopper as a one-person sourcing and trading business in China. I source from suppliers and resell to overseas customers; I don’t manufacture the goods myself.

Can you source items outside these categories?

Yes, you’re welcome to enquire about other products available in China. I check the specific item and destination before confirming whether I can help.

Can I send a Taobao, 1688 or other seller link?

Yes. A seller link helps identify the item. If you only have a photo or description, share that instead. I’ll discuss whether a suitable match can be found.

Is there a minimum order?

Tell me the quantity you need, whether it’s one item or a business order. Minimum quantities depend on the seller, product and any custom requirements.

How much will it cost?

Each request is quoted individually. Product cost, service fees, shipping and possible destination charges should be clarified before purchase. This website does not display live prices or stock.

Can you ship to my country?

Send your delivery country and product details. Available routes, restrictions, transit estimates and destination requirements vary by item and location.
