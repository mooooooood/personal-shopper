    THE RABBIT MEADOW

    MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

    Small lives.
    Simple pleasures.

    Everyone visits the same little world. Rabbits, carrots and the basket are shared. Grown-up rabbits meet, share a heart and welcome a baby with a randomly chosen coat: snowy white, caramel, charcoal, spotted and more.

    Eight seats, one meadow. Join a numbered seat at the edge of the grass and watch other players cast and reel in their own ropes. Your seat has a matching colour and a basket to aim for. Open the online count to see each seat’s activity. These are real visitors, with no computer players filling empty seats.

    If all eight seats are taken, you can watch until a place opens; the page will try to join you automatically. A short absence has a 20-second grace period. An unfinished rope keeps its seat reserved until it is released or expires. No sign-up is needed.

    Watch
    See who’s hopping around. Tap a rabbit to say hello.
    Carrot
    Tap the grass to leave a treat for everyone’s rabbits.
    Lasso
    Choose Lasso and tap near a rabbit once. The loop flies to that fixed spot in about a second, while the rabbit keeps moving. Lead its hop, or tempt it to stay with a carrot. A landed loop automatically reels it towards your seat’s basket; a miss comes back empty. No holding or second click needed. Let go unties the rope.
    Release
    Bring one rabbit out of the shared basket and back onto the grass.

    A rabbit needs at least 4.5 seconds of pulling to reach the basket. The rope slips free after 25 seconds, so keep an eye on the progress bar. Everyone can see your rope. It reels in for you automatically; only you can untie it.

    Little underground shortcuts. Five grassy burrows connect the meadow. Rabbits sometimes hop into one, disappear for a moment and peek out of a randomly chosen entrance. Everyone sees the same journey. A rabbit in a burrow cannot be lassoed or taken by wildlife; it still counts as part of the meadow.

    Your field journal. Bring each of the eight coat colours safely home to earn its first-catch stamp. Only your own successful catches observed by this browser count. The book stays in this browser; it does not follow you to another device.

    The seat baskets all lead to the same shared basket. Catching a rabbit keeps it safe for everyone; it does not become a private item.

    Wild visitors give you a three-second warning: an eagle’s shadow glides across the grass, or a wolf rustles in the bushes. The animal appears when the chase begins. An eagle or grey wolf can steal a rabbit while you are pulling it in. Your rabbit is safe only once it reaches the basket. Each visit takes at most one rabbit and leaves at least two on the grass.

    Pause freezes only your view and disables feeding, lassoing and releasing. Pausing, opening a panel or switching tabs rests your pull. A loop already in flight still lands in the shared world. Returning to the active meadow automatically resumes your unfinished pull before the rope expires. Play brings you back to the latest shared state. Other visitors can keep playing while you take a break.

    Sync fetches the latest shared state. Refreshing the page or returning later keeps the meadow’s progress. When nobody is watching, the meadow rests until someone returns.

    If the connection drops, interactions are disabled while the page reconnects. Your view will catch up when the connection returns.

    Playing with a keyboard? Focus the map and use the arrow keys to move the cursor. W / C / N select Watch, Carrot or Lasso; Enter or Space uses the tool. Press once to cast at the cursor; a landed loop reels in automatically. You do not need to hold a key. Press Escape to close this window.

    There’s no score. Just one little world to share.
